About This Project

BRIGHT HOMES, LOWER BILLS AND ENERGY SAVING is a Community Service Project designed to create awareness among households about:

  • Electricity consumption patterns
  • Energy conservation practices
  • Rooftop solar adoption
  • Available government support schemes

This tool helps households understand their energy usage, explore solar options, and learn about government initiatives like PM Surya Ghar.
