Registration Guidance

Step-by-step guide for PM Surya Ghar registration

⚠️ Important: This is a general guidance document. The actual registration process must be completed through the official government portal. This application cannot process actual registrations or subsidy applications.

📝 Step-by-Step Registration Process

1

Check Eligibility & Gather Information

Verify your eligibility for the scheme. Ensure you have a valid electricity connection and necessary documents. Check your electricity consumer number and recent bills.

2

Prepare Required Documents

Gather necessary documents including electricity bill, identity proof (Aadhaar), property ownership documents or NOC, bank account details, and rooftop photographs.

3

Visit Official Government Portal

Visit the official PM Surya Ghar portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) to begin the registration process. Create an account or log in if you already have one.

4

Complete Application Form

Fill in the application form with accurate details about your household, electricity connection, and rooftop information. Upload required documents as specified.

5

Select Approved Vendor

Choose an empaneled vendor from the list of approved solar installers provided on the portal. Compare options and select a vendor as per your preference.

6

Complete Installation

Coordinate with the selected vendor to complete the rooftop solar installation. Ensure the installation meets technical standards and safety requirements.

7

Inspection & Net Metering

After installation, the system will be inspected by concerned authorities. Complete the net metering process with your electricity distribution company.

8

Track Application & Subsidy

Monitor your application status through the official portal. Upon successful completion of all steps, the subsidy will be disbursed as per scheme guidelines.

📌 Important Notes

  • Always use the official government portal for registration
  • Provide accurate and truthful information in the application
  • Keep copies of all submitted documents and application receipts
  • Follow up regularly on your application status
  • Contact the helpline provided on the portal for queries
  • Be aware of fraudulent agents or unofficial portals
  • Understand the terms and conditions before proceeding
