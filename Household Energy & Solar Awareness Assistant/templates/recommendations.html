Energy Saving Recommendations

Personalized suggestions to reduce your electricity consumption

ℹ️ Note: These recommendations are based on general energy-saving practices and your appliance usage. Actual savings may vary.

⚡ Quick Savings

Immediate actions you can take today

Switch off appliances when not in use

Turn off lights, fans, and other appliances when leaving a room.

Potential saving: 5-10%
Avoid standby power

Unplug devices or use smart power strips to eliminate standby power consumption.

Potential saving: 3-5%

🔧 Low-Cost Improvements

Affordable upgrades with good returns

Replace with LED bulbs

Replace incandescent bulbs with LED bulbs which use 75% less energy.

Potential saving: 10-15%
Use natural lighting

Maximize use of natural daylight during daytime hours.

Potential saving: 5-8%
Optimize refrigerator usage

Maintain appropriate temperature (3-4°C) and avoid frequent door opening.

Potential saving: 5-10%

🏠 Long-Term Improvements

Investments for significant long-term savings

Energy-efficient appliances

Consider upgrading to 5-star rated energy-efficient appliances.

Potential saving: 15-30%
Install rooftop solar

Consider installing rooftop solar panels to generate clean energy and reduce electricity bills.

Potential saving: 40-70%
Improve home insulation

Better insulation reduces heating and cooling requirements.

Potential saving: 10-20%

💡 Additional Energy Tips

  • Use ceiling fans instead of air conditioning when possible
  • Set air conditioner temperature to 24-26°C for optimal efficiency
  • Run washing machines and dishwashers with full loads
  • Use cold water for laundry when suitable
  • Dry clothes naturally instead of using dryers
  • Regularly maintain appliances for optimal performance
  • Use timers for water heaters and geysers
  • Seal windows and doors to prevent air leaks
⚠️ Important Note

These recommendations are general guidelines based on energy-saving best practices. Potential savings are estimates and actual results may vary based on individual circumstances, implementation quality, and usage patterns. Consult with energy professionals for personalized advice.
