PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Government Rooftop Solar Scheme

⚠️ Important Notice: Scheme benefits, eligibility criteria, subsidy amounts, and procedures may change. Always verify the latest information through official government sources before applying.

🇮🇳 About the Scheme

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a government initiative aimed at promoting rooftop solar installations across India. The scheme provides financial support in the form of subsidies to households for installing solar panels on their rooftops.

The objective is to increase the adoption of solar energy, reduce electricity bills for households, and contribute to India's renewable energy targets.

🏠

For Households

Residential households can avail subsidies for installing rooftop solar systems to generate their own electricity.

💰

Subsidy Support

Financial assistance is provided to reduce the upfront cost of solar installation for eligible households.

🔌

Net Metering

Excess electricity generated can be fed back to the grid, earning credits or reducing future bills.

🌱

Clean Energy

Promotes the use of renewable energy and reduces dependence on conventional electricity sources.

📋 Eligibility (General Guidelines)

Note: Eligibility criteria may vary. Please check official sources for current requirements.
  • Residential households with valid electricity connection
  • Owns the rooftop or has necessary permissions
  • Rooftop should be suitable for solar installation
  • Should meet the technical requirements specified by the scheme
  • Should not have already availed similar subsidies for the same premises

📝 Application Process Overview

Step 1: Check Eligibility

Verify that you meet the eligibility criteria for the scheme.

Step 2: Register on Portal

Visit the official government portal and complete the registration process with your details.

Step 3: Select Vendor

Choose an empaneled vendor from the list of approved installers.

Step 4: Installation

The vendor will install the rooftop solar system at your premises.

Step 5: Inspection & Net Metering

After installation, inspection and net metering process is completed.

Step 6: Subsidy Disbursement

Upon successful completion, subsidy is disbursed as per scheme guidelines.

📄 Documents Generally Required

Note: Document requirements may vary. Please verify current requirements on the official portal.
  • Electricity bill / Consumer number
  • Aadhaar Card or other identity proof
  • Property ownership documents or NOC
  • Bank account details for subsidy transfer
  • Recent photograph of the rooftop
  • Any other documents as specified by the scheme
